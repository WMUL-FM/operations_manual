10.I. Signing the Station On and Off the Air

10.I.1. Signing The Station Off the Air

WMUL‑FM operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. However, it is occasionally necessary to sign off the transmitter and cease on-air operations. During these times, programming will continue on the webstream. Someone from the engineering staff will direct you to sign the station off when needed.

These instructions presume that programming is playing through Studio A — Rivendell 1.

  1. Have a pen and a piece of note paper handy.

  2. Place "Rivdell 1" in AUD.

  3. On the router:

    Destinations Sources

    Mux Strm
    (Music Stream)

    ←

    A — PGM

    Xmitter
    (Transmitter)

    ←

    A — AUD

  4. Place "Panel 1" in AUD only, fade it up, and turn it on.

  5. Locate the sign-off cart in the computer playback system library. Three different sign off carts exist for different sign-off durations. If you are uncertain which cart to use, play the "A Few Hours" cart.

    • MISC — 040001 — One Hour

    • MISC — 040002 — Two Hours

    • MISC — 040003 — "A Few" Hours

  6. Load the sign-off cart into a Sound Panel button.

  7. Take a meter reading within 5 minutes of sign off.

  8. When the song ends, take "Rivdell 1" out of AUD. This will stop music from going to the transmitter. Music will continue on the webstream.

  9. Look at the Modulation Monitor. Make certain that the left and right channels are showing zero.

  10. Play the sign-off cart from the Sound Panel.

  11. On the note paper, record "Program Off:" and the time the sign-off cart finished playing.

  12. On the Transmitter Remote:

    1. Scroll to "Channel 4 — On / Off".

    2. Press the Red "Xmit Off" button for 1 second. The wattage displayed should drop to zero over a few seconds.

    3. Check the Modulation Monitor. The "Multipath" meter should read full and the signal level meter should drop to zero.

    4. If needed, push the "Xmit Off" button again.

  13. On the note paper, record "Carrier Off:" and the time the watt reading dropped to zero.

  14. Inform the engineering staff that the station is signed off. Include the times you recorded on your note paper.

  15. Turn off the modulation monitor to prevent the silence alarm from blaring through the station.

10.I.2. Signing the Station On the Air

After maintenance is completed, the engineering staff will direct you to sign the station back on.

Steps 2-4 should still be completed from the sign-off, but check them to make certain.

  1. Have a pen and a piece of note paper handy.

  2. Make certain that "Rivdell 1" is not in AUD.

  3. On the router:

    Destinations Sources

    Xmitter
    (Transmitter)

    ←

    A — AUD

  4. Place "Panel 1" in AUD only, fade it up, and turn it on.

  5. On the Transmitter Remote:

    1. Scroll to "Channel 4 — On / Off".

    2. Press the Green "Xmit On" button for 1 second. The wattage displayed should raise to approximately 3230 watts over a few seconds.

  6. If needed, push the "Xmit On" button again.

  7. On the note paper, record "Carrier On:" and the time the watt reading reached 3230 watts.

  8. Give the transmitter a minute to warm up.

  9. Turn on the modulation monitor. Give it several seconds and confirm that the "Multipath" meter reads zero and the signal level meter is full.

  10. Locate the sign-on cut in the computer playback library. It should be MISC — 040000.

  11. Load the sign-on cart into a Sound Panel button.

  12. Look at the Button Log in RD AirPlay. When there is approximately forty-eight (48) seconds remaining on the current song, play the sign-on cart from the Sound Panel button. The sign-on cart is also forty-eight (48) seconds long. Waiting until there is only forty-eight (48) seconds left in the current song helps smooth the on-air transition from the sign-on cart to the song.

  13. When the cut finishes, put "Rivdell 1" in AUD. This will cause music to resume playing over the air.

  14. On the note paper, record "Program On:" and the time the sign-on started playing.

  15. Inform the engineering staff that the station is signed on. Include the times you recorded on your note paper.

  16. Take a meter reading within 5 minutes of signing on.

  17. On the router:

    Destinations Sources

    Xmitter
    (Transmitter)

    ←

    A — PGM

    Mux Strm
    (Music Stream)

    ←

    WMUL Pst

  18. Take "Rivdell 1" and "Panel 1" out of AUD.
