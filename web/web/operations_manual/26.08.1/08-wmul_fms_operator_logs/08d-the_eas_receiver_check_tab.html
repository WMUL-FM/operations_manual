8.D. The EAS Receiver Check Tab

Click on the "EAS Receiver Check" tab button to display the "EAS Receiver Check" tab. This log provides a record of the EAS receiver.

A daily EAS Receiver Check must be completed at least once every broadcast day. The first operator of the calendar day is the first person responsible for the receiver check. If the first operator fails to complete the check, the duty falls to the next operator, and so on. Usually, the first operator of the new broadcast day is the morning show. However, your airshift might begin at 11:00 P.M. and you might still be the first operator for that broadcast day. If that is so, it is your duty to perform the check.

The EAS Receiver Check tab consists of two sections. The first part (1) shows when the last EAS Receiver Check was performed. In the screenshot, "David Adkins" performed the check August 03, 2023 at 05:17:40 PM EDT.

The second part (2-4) is where new checks are entered. There are three sets of radio buttons (2) [one for each source], a notes field (3), and a button to record the information (4).

8.D.1. How to Complete the Daily EAS Receiver Check

Check the "EAS Receiver Check" tab. If the date of the most recent check is any day but today, a new check is needed. (In the screenshot, the most recent check was August 03, 2023. If it’s still August 03, 2023, nothing more needs to be done.)

  1. On the board, double-check that "Editor" (#17-A) is turned off. You are about to play audio from the editor computer and you do not want it to go over the air.

  2. On the Studio A — Editor computer, adjust the volume knob on right-hand side of the speaker bar to make certain the audio won’t blast at an uncomfortable volume.

  3. Minimize everything so that the desktop is visible.

  4. Double-click on the "EAS Receiver" icon on the desktop. The EAS Receiver WebUI will open in a new tab in Firefox. (Sometimes there will be a bookmark on the Bookmarks bar inside Firefox, but that is not guaranteed. The icon on the desktop is guaranteed.)

  5. It may ask for a username and password. The username can be left blank. The password is available in the group chat (Microsoft Teams).

  6. There is a lot of information on this page, but the only part of the page that you have to worry about is the top right-hand corner. There is a speaker button and a dropdown box in that corner. It is highlighted in the screenshot above.

    The speaker icon turns audio playback on and off. The dropdown box selects which of the three radio stations is playing. In the screenshot, "WDGG 93.7" is selected.

  7. If "WDGG 93.7" is not already selected, use the dropdown box to select it.

  8. Click the speaker icon to begin listening to country music from "The DAWG". If needed, adjust the volume using the volume knob on the right-hand side of the speaker bar.

  9. On the "wmul_log" tab: select the appropriate radio button in the "Channel 1 (WDGG 93.7 FM — The DAWG)" column (Item 2, left-hand column).

  10. On the "WMUL-EASTUNER" tab: use the dropdown box to select "ESPN 930" and listen for sports/talk programming from AM-930.

  11. On the "wmul_log" tab: select the appropriate radio button in the "Channel 2 (WRVC 930 AM — ESPN 930)" column (Item 2, center column).

  12. On the "WMUL-EASTUNER" tab: use the dropdown box to select "NWS Charleston" and listen for the National Weather Service’s robot reading the weather.

  13. Click the speaker icon to stop playback.

  14. On the "wmul_log" tab: select the appropriate radio button in the "Channel 3 — NWS in Charleston" column (Item 2, right-hand column).

  15. If any of the stations were not working, make a note in the "Notes" box (3) as to how it isn’t working. E.G. "Channel 2 is completely silent" or "Channel 3 is static".

  16. Click the "Complete Receiver Check" button (4). The "Most Recent Check" line (1) will be updated.

  17. You may close out the "WMUL-EASTUNER" tab if you wish.
